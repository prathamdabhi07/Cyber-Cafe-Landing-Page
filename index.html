About the centre

A Common Service Centre built for Palitana

Knowledge World Online is a locally run Digital Seva Kendra offering government-to-citizen (G2C) services, financial inclusion services and everyday office support — Aadhaar, PAN, certificates, bill payments and same-day printing — under a single, trusted counter.

Every request, from a certificate application to a print job, is logged with a token number so you always know where things stand.
